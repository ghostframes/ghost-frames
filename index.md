# Ghost Frames Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026

Ghost Frames is a drawing and animation app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **Ghost Frames does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.**

## Information we collect

None. Ghost Frames has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The app does not send your drawings, recordings, settings, or any other information to us or to anyone else.

## Your work stays on your device

Everything you create in Ghost Frames, including drawings, animations, sound recordings, palettes, stamps, themes, and settings, is stored only on your device. If you have device backups turned on (for example, iCloud Backup), your app data may be included in those backups, which are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Microphone:** used only when you choose to record sound onto your animation. Recordings are saved on your device and are never uploaded.
- **Photos (add only):** used only to save the images and animations you export to your photo library. Ghost Frames cannot see or read the photos already in your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

## Sharing

Ghost Frames only shares something when you choose to, for example when you export an animation and send it with the share sheet. What happens after that depends on the app or service you share it with.

## Purchases

Ghost Frames Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, payment details, or other personal information about your purchase.

## Children's privacy

Ghost Frames does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us through the [Ghost Frames support page](support/).
