# Ghost Frames Support

Need help with Ghost Frames, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **[hello.ghostframes@yahoo.com](mailto:hello.ghostframes@yahoo.com)**.

If you're reporting a problem, it helps to include:

- your device (for example, iPhone 15 or iPad Air)
- your iOS or iPadOS version
- what you were doing when it happened, and what you expected instead
- a screenshot or screen recording, if you can

## Common questions

**How do I learn the basics?**
Open **help > tutorial** in the app to replay the walkthrough at any time. You can also tap **?** and then any tool or menu item to see what it does.

**I bought Ghost Frames Pro. How do I get it back on a new device?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Pro upgrade screen in the app, and tap **restore**.

**Where are my drawings saved?**
On your device only. Ghost Frames doesn't upload your work anywhere. Deleting the app also deletes your drawings, so export anything you want to keep first.

**How do I save or share an animation?**
Use **file > export** to save a png, gif, sprite sheet, mp4 or mov to Photos or share it with another app.

## Privacy

Ghost Frames doesn't collect any personal data. Read the full [privacy policy](../).
